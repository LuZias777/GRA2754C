NESTLÉ COMPLAINT HANDLING PROCEDURE

If you have consumed a product manufactured, imported, and/or marketed by us and wish to file a complaint, we suggest that you contact us immediately through any of the following Consumer Service channels:

Call Us

Toll-free line: 0800 – 10210 (accessible from landlines only).

Service Hours: Monday to Friday, 9:00 a.m. – 6:00 p.m.

Write to Us

Email: servicioalconsumidor@pe.nestle.com

Service Hours: Monday to Friday, 9:00 a.m. – 6:00 p.m.

Direct Message

Get in touch with us via our official social media channels.

FACEBOOK

Upon receiving your complaint—whether via our toll-free line or email—we will initiate our Consumer Service procedure. For this purpose, and subject to your express consent, we will require the following information:

If you have a sample of the product in question:

1. Data Collection

If you call our telephone service line, our representatives will collect the information necessary to register your complaint. If you have submitted a complaint regarding our products via email, we will request your contact phone numbers, as well as the following:

  • Personal Information:
    • Full name, identification document details, or contact information.
    • Address, phone numbers, and email address.
  • Complaint Details:
    • Product Name, Expiration Date, or Batch Number
    • Place of purchase
    • Quantity purchased, date of purchase, and date the product was opened.
    • Observations regarding the product.
2. Sample Collection

To address your concern, it is necessary to evaluate the product sample you have. For this purpose, our courier—who will be properly identified—will visit the address you provide to collect the product and transport it to the Quality Assurance Department at our Lima factory, where our specialists will conduct the necessary evaluations. The courier will take photographs of the product provided by the consumer to document the condition of the sample. Providing a sample is voluntary and remains at the consumer's discretion.

3. Response to Your Complaint

Once the analysis of the product has been completed, we will contact you via telephone and/or email to provide the response prepared by our team of specialists.

If you have the product sample, you will receive a response within a maximum of 15 business days, in accordance with Peruvian legal regulations. If you do not have the product sample, we will be unable to perform an analysis. Nevertheless, we will record the case in our system—including the product information and details of the complaint—so that the Quality Assurance Department is informed and can conduct an analysis of counter-samples.

For any questions regarding our complaint-handling procedure, or if you wish to share any further observations, please contact us via our nationwide toll-free line: 0800 – 10210. Our service hours are Monday through Friday, from 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.

Please note that NESTLÉ PERÚ S.A. aligns its Consumer Service procedures with the current Consumer Protection Code. We advise all our consumers to keep in mind the regulations on this matter.
